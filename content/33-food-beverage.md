## FAQ

- **Question:** Does it work at the counter and online?
  - **Answer:** Yes. QRIS and cards at the counter, payment links in chat and FLIK Checkout on your site all land in the same FLIK balance.
- **Question:** What do I need at the counter?
  - **Answer:** A FLIK device for each outlet. It shows QRIS on the screen, takes cards and prints the receipt.
- **Question:** Can you deliver within the city?
  - **Answer:** Yes. Book instant delivery by motorbike, or same day, from the FLIK dashboard.
- **Question:** Can FLIK help me open a new outlet?
  - **Answer:** Upfront funding is available for selected brands, settled from your sales. Talk to the FLIK team to see if you're eligible.
- **Question:** Is FLIK a bank?
  - **Answer:** No. Payments are processed by licensed payment partners.
