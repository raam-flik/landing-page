## FAQ

- **Question:** Does FLIK work with my fashion store?
  - **Answer:** Yes, if you sell on Shopify or WooCommerce. FLIK sets up FLIK Checkout for you. You can also sell with payment links and in store, with no website.
- **Question:** Can shoppers pay by COD?
  - **Answer:** Yes. COD is one of the payment methods, with a 3% fee (excl. PPN) deducted after delivery.
- **Question:** Can I sell a drop as a pre-order?
  - **Answer:** Yes. Share a payment link in chat or in your bio, with your own button text, like Pre-order now.
- **Question:** Can I take payments at a pop-up or store?
  - **Answer:** Yes. The FLIK EDC takes QRIS and cards, and in-store sales land in the same balance as online.
- **Question:** Can I ship a whole drop at once?
  - **Answer:** Yes. Bulk shipping sends orders from all your warehouses to many destinations at once, with every major courier.
- **Question:** Can I fund stock for the season?
  - **Answer:** Selected brands can get a Merchant Cash Advance for one fixed margin, settled automatically from sales.
