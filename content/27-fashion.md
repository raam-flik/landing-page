## FAQ

- **Question:** Does FLIK work with my fashion store?
  - **Answer:** Yes, if you sell on Shopify or WooCommerce. FLIK sets up FLIK Checkout for you.
- **Question:** Can shoppers pay by COD?
  - **Answer:** Yes. COD is one of the payment methods, with a 3% fee (excl. PPN) deducted after delivery.
- **Question:** Can I fund stock for the season?
  - **Answer:** Selected brands can get a Merchant Cash Advance, settled automatically as products sell.
