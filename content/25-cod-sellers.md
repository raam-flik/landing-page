## FAQ

- **Question:** How much is the COD fee?
  - **Answer:** 3% of the COD amount, plus PPN. It is deducted when the COD amount is credited after delivery, so you never pay it upfront.
- **Question:** When do I get the COD money?
  - **Answer:** It is credited to your FLIK balance after delivery.
- **Question:** What if the shopper refuses the parcel?
  - **Answer:** The courier tries again the next day, up to two attempts, then returns it. No COD fee is charged on a returned parcel.
- **Question:** Which couriers can I use?
  - **Answer:** All major couriers, compared in one dashboard.
