## FAQ

- **Question:** How much is the COD fee?
  - **Answer:** 3% of the COD amount, plus PPN. It is deducted when the COD money is credited after delivery, so you never pay it upfront.
- **Question:** When do I get the COD money?
  - **Answer:** It is credited to your FLIK balance as soon as the parcel is delivered.
- **Question:** Which couriers support COD?
  - **Answer:** JNE, J&amp;T, SiCepat, SPX Express, ID Express, AnterAja and Lion Parcel.
- **Question:** What if the shopper refuses the parcel?
  - **Answer:** The courier tries again the next day, up to two attempts, then returns it. No COD fee is charged on a returned parcel. The return fee for each courier is shown in your FLIK dashboard.
- **Question:** When is my order picked up?
  - **Answer:** The same day, if the order is ready by 14:00 WIB.
- **Question:** Can shoppers pay before delivery instead?
  - **Answer:** Yes. Send a payment link, and they pay by QRIS, e-wallet, virtual account or card.
- **Question:** Can FLIK flag risky COD orders?
  - **Answer:** Coming soon: a risk level for each order, WhatsApp confirmation before pickup and an address check.
- **Question:** How do I withdraw my money?
  - **Answer:** Withdraw to your bank from Rp100.000, for Rp4.000 + PPN, or settle early when you need cash sooner.
