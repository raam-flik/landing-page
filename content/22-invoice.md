## FAQ

- **Question:** How does my customer pay an invoice?
  - **Answer:** They open the link in the invoice email and pay on a FLIK payment page with QRIS, an e-wallet, a bank virtual account or a card. Customers with a FLIK account can pay with FLIK in a few taps.
- **Question:** When is the invoice due?
  - **Answer:** You choose: 7, 14, 30 or 60 days after you send it. After the due date the invoice shows as Past due.
- **Question:** Can I remind a customer?
  - **Answer:** Yes. Send a WhatsApp reminder from an open or past due invoice, with the same payment link.
- **Question:** Does my customer get a PDF?
  - **Answer:** Yes. The invoice email has the PDF attached, and your customer can download the invoice and the receipt after paying.
- **Question:** Do I have to match payments to invoices?
  - **Answer:** No. Each invoice is paid on its own payment page, so FLIK marks the right invoice Paid.
- **Question:** Where does the money go?
  - **Answer:** Into your FLIK balance, the same balance as your other FLIK payments. Withdraw to your bank from Rp100.000, or settle early when you need cash sooner.
- **Question:** What does it cost?
  - **Answer:** There is no setup fee. Each paid invoice costs the rate of the payment method your customer used, shown on the Pricing page.
- **Question:** Do I need to verify my business?
  - **Answer:** Yes. Verify your identity and business before you send invoices.
