## FAQ

- **Question:** How does my customer pay an invoice?
  - **Answer:** They open the link in the email and pay on a FLIK payment page with QRIS, an e-wallet, a bank virtual account or a card.
- **Question:** When is the invoice due?
  - **Answer:** You choose: 7, 14, 30 or 60 days after you send it.
- **Question:** Can I remind a customer?
  - **Answer:** Yes. Send a reminder from the invoice in the dashboard.
- **Question:** Where does the money go?
  - **Answer:** Into your FLIK balance, the same balance as your other FLIK payments.
- **Question:** What does it cost?
  - **Answer:** The rate of the payment method your customer uses, as shown on the Pricing page, plus PPN.
- **Question:** Do I need to verify my business?
  - **Answer:** Yes. Verify your identity and business before you send invoices.
