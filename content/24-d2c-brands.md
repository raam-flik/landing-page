## FAQ

- **Question:** Which stores does FLIK work with?
  - **Answer:** Shopify and WooCommerce. FLIK sets up FLIK Checkout for you.
- **Question:** Do shoppers need a FLIK account?
  - **Answer:** No. They can check out as a guest, or create a FLIK account so their details are saved for every store that uses FLIK.
- **Question:** What does it cost?
  - **Answer:** A success fee of 0–2% (excl. PPN) on each successful payment, for the whole checkout. Shipping is paid per shipment.
- **Question:** Can I fund stock for a drop?
  - **Answer:** Selected brands can get a Merchant Cash Advance, settled automatically from sales.
