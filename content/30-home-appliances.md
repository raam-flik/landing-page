## FAQ

- **Question:** Can shoppers pay a large amount by bank transfer?
  - **Answer:** Yes. Each order gets its own virtual account number at BCA, Mandiri, BNI, BRI or Permata, so the payment is matched to the order automatically.
- **Question:** Can you ship heavy and bulky items?
  - **Answer:** Yes. Choose cargo, such as J&amp;T Cargo or JNE Trucking, for heavy and bulky items at a lower rate per kg, or same day and instant in the city.
- **Question:** Can I take payments in my showroom?
  - **Answer:** Yes. The FLIK EDC takes cards and QRIS, and showroom sales land in the same balance as online.
- **Question:** Can business customers pay by invoice?
  - **Answer:** Yes. Send an invoice with a due date of 7, 14, 30 or 60 days, your NPWP and a PO number. It is paid online and marked paid for you.
- **Question:** Can shoppers pay on delivery?
  - **Answer:** Yes. COD is available with a 3% fee (excl. PPN), deducted after delivery.
- **Question:** Can I fund stock before a big sale?
  - **Answer:** Selected brands can get a Merchant Cash Advance for one fixed margin, settled automatically from sales.
