## 1. About this policy
<!-- html: 1. About this policy -->

- **Text:** This policy is from **PT Flik Teknologi Indonesia** (“**FLIK**”, “**we**”). It applies to orders paid through FLIK Checkout, parcels sent with FLIK Shipping, and fees that merchants pay FLIK.
- **Text:** Merchants sell the goods. FLIK provides the checkout and shipping technology. Payments are processed by licensed payment partners. FLIK is not a bank. Partners: [[P: name the licensed payment partners and their licences]]
- **Text:** **Language.** This page is in English for now. A Bahasa Indonesia version will follow. If the two versions differ, the Bahasa Indonesia version prevails.

## 2. Refunds for shoppers
<!-- html: 2. Refunds for shoppers -->

- **Text:** **The merchant decides.** The store that sold you the goods handles returns and refunds, under its own return policy. Contact the merchant first.
- **Text:** **FLIK processes the refund on the merchant's instruction.** When the merchant approves a refund, we send it through the original payment method where possible. A refund can be full or partial. Refunds on one order never exceed what you paid.
- **Text:** **If the original method can't take a refund**, we send it by bank transfer to an account you give us. We use your bank details only for that refund. [[W-30: which methods can't take a refund]]
- **Text:** **Timing.** After the merchant approves, we send the refund to the payment partner within [[W-31: FLIK processing time]]. When you see the money depends on the method: [[W-32: typical time per method]].
- **Text:** **If you've also raised a dispute** with your bank or card issuer, we pause the refund until that dispute is settled, so you aren't refunded twice.
- **Text:** **If you can't reach the merchant**, or disagree with its answer, contact FLIK support (section 6). We'll pass your complaint to the merchant and follow it up. [[W-33: what FLIK commits to, and response time]] We aim to resolve refund requests promptly: [[target time, for counsel]]

## 3. COD orders and returned parcels
<!-- html: 3. COD orders and returned parcels -->

- **List item:** **Cash on delivery (COD):** you pay the courier when the parcel arrives. If you refuse the parcel, you pay nothing and it goes back to the merchant.
- **List item:** **Refund of a COD order after delivery:** the merchant decides. There's no card or e-wallet to refund to, so the refund goes by bank transfer to an account you give us. [[W-35: other refund methods for COD]]
- **List item:** **Failed delivery:** the courier tries again the next day, up to two attempts. Courier-direct services don't retry. The parcel then goes back to the merchant.
- **List item:** **Prepaid order returned undelivered:** [[W-36: automatic refund, or merchant approval]]
- **List item:** **Lost or damaged parcels:** FLIK files a claim with the courier. When the courier pays, we pass the compensation for the goods to the merchant. The merchant handles your refund. [[W-37: courier claim caps and filing deadlines]]

## 4. Refunds of FLIK's fees (for merchants)
<!-- html: 4. Refunds of FLIK's fees (for merchants) -->

- **Table columns:** Fee — When it's refunded
- **Table row:** Registration — Registration is free, so there's nothing to refund.
- **Table row:** Checkout payment fee (MDR) — Charged when the shopper pays. It isn't returned when an order is refunded.
- **Table row:** Checkout platform (success) fee — Earned when the shopper's payment succeeds. It isn't refunded, including when the order is refunded or the parcel isn't delivered.
- **Table row:** Payment partner refund fee — If a payment partner charges a fee to process a refund, or a bank transfer is needed, that cost is charged to you. [[W-39: fees per method]]
- **Table row:** Shipping fee, shipment cancelled before pickup — [[W-40: refunded or not]]
- **Table row:** Shipping fee, parcel returned to you — [[W-41: refunded or not]]. A return fee may also apply: [[W-42: return fee per courier]]
- **Table row:** COD fee (3%, excl. PPN) — Paid by the merchant: 3% (excl. PPN), deducted when the COD amount is credited after delivery. No COD fee is charged on a returned parcel, so there's nothing to refund.
- **Table row:** Shipping fee, lost or damaged parcel — Refunded only if the courier refunds it, once its payment reaches us. FLIK keeps its platform fee once the parcel has been picked up.
- **Table row:** Merchant Cash Advance margin — [[W-45: any refund on early settlement]]
- **Table row:** Fee charged in error — Tell us within [[W-43: deadline]]. We check it and correct it [[W-43: to your FLIK balance or bank account]].
- **Text:** Money in your FLIK balance that you haven't used can be withdrawn. [[W-26: withdrawal timing, minimum and fee]]

## 5. How to ask for a refund
<!-- html: 5. How to ask for a refund -->

- **List item:** **Shoppers:** contact the merchant first. If that doesn't work, contact FLIK support (section 6). Tell us the order number, the store and what went wrong.
- **List item:** **Merchants refunding an order:** use the merchant dashboard. [[W-46: confirm the refund action is live; until then, through FLIK support]]
- **List item:** **Merchants with a question about a fee:** contact FLIK support with the order, shipment (AWB) or invoice number.
