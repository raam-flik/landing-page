## FAQ

- **Question:** Do you offer volume pricing?
  - **Answer:** Yes. Pricing is set around your volume and the products you use. Talk to sales and we'll prepare it with you.
- **Question:** Can FLIK work with our existing systems?
  - **Answer:** Yes. Connect your order system, ERP or app through the Merchant API, use our Shopify and WooCommerce set-up, or run it all from the FLIK dashboard.
- **Question:** Who helps us get set up?
  - **Answer:** A named FLIK team, from onboarding and going live to the day-to-day questions after.
- **Question:** Can we take payments online and in store?
  - **Answer:** Yes. Checkout, invoices, payment links and in-store payments all land in the same FLIK balance.
- **Question:** Is FLIK a bank?
  - **Answer:** No. Payments are processed by licensed payment partners.
