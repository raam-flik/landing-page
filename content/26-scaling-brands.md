## FAQ

- **Question:** Who can get a Merchant Cash Advance?
  - **Answer:** It is offered to selected merchants after an assessment. Businesses usually need at least 6 months of sales.
- **Question:** What does a Cash Advance cost?
  - **Answer:** One fixed margin, set in your offer and agreed before you sign. It never changes, and there is no compounding interest.
- **Question:** How is it settled?
  - **Answer:** A share of each sale goes toward settlement automatically, so slower sales mean smaller settlements.
- **Question:** Can I ship from more than one warehouse?
  - **Answer:** Yes. Bulk shipping sends orders from all your warehouses to many destinations at once.
- **Question:** Can physical stores use FLIK?
  - **Answer:** Yes. Each outlet gets its own FLIK EDC for QRIS and cards, and in-store sales land in the same balance as online.
- **Question:** Can I get paid sooner?
  - **Answer:** Yes. Early Settlement pays out before the standard settlement date, for 0.2% of each non-card payment or 0.3% of each card payment, plus PPN.
