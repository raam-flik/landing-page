## 1. What you can ask for
<!-- html: 1. What you can ask for -->

- **Text:** Under Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection (UU PDP)†, you can ask us to:
- **List item:** **give you access** to your personal data and a copy of it (Art. 7†);
- **List item:** **correct** data that's wrong or incomplete (Art. 6†);
- **List item:** **delete** your personal data (Art. 8†);
- **List item:** **withdraw your consent** for a use based on consent, such as marketing (Art. 9†);
- **List item:** **object** to a use of your data, including a decision made only by automated processing (Art. 10†, Art. 11†).
- **Text:** Your other rights are in our [Privacy Policy](./privacy/), section 9.
- **Text:** **Language.** This page is in English for now. A Bahasa Indonesia version will follow. If the two versions differ, the Bahasa Indonesia version prevails.

## 2. Before you start
<!-- html: 2. Before you start -->

- **List item:** Anyone can use this form: shoppers, merchants and website visitors.
- **List item:** **Shoppers:** if your request is about data a store holds, such as its customer list or its own promotions, contact that store too. We'll help pass your request on.
- **List item:** We only ask for what we need to find your data and check it's you. **Don't include ID numbers, documents or passwords.**

<!-- html: 4. What happens next -->
