## 8. FAQ
<!-- html: FAQ (single column) #faq-sec -->

- **Section heading:** Questions, answered.
- **Button:** What is a Merchant Cash Advance?
- **Answer:** Upfront funding for your business. FLIK provides the money to stock inventory and grow, and settlement is deducted automatically from your product sales.
- **Button:** Who is it for?
- **Answer:** Selected brand owners who sell online, in physical stores, or both. For example on their own Shopify or WooCommerce store, with FLIK Checkout, or through a cashier system in their outlets.
- **Button:** How do I know if I am eligible?
- **Answer:** Start with a consultation. The FLIK team learns about your business, and we may ask for some business information to decide whether you are eligible.
- **Button:** What can I use the funding for?
- **Answer:** Growth: buying inventory, marketing campaigns, opening a new outlet, or keeping cash flow steady through busy seasons.
- **Button:** How is it settled?
- **Answer:** You sell as usual. A share of each sale goes toward settlement automatically, and you can track the outstanding amount and the latest payments in your dashboard.
- **Button:** What does it cost?
- **Answer:** A monthly platform fee (Biaya Platform) as a % of the funded amount for the funding period, set in each offer, plus a one-time 1% administration fee per placement. All fees exclude PPN. Terms are set out in each offer letter.
- **Button:** Do I give up ownership of my business?
- **Answer:** No. FLIK funds your stock, not your shares, so you keep full ownership of your brand.
- **Button:** How long does it take?
- **Answer:** The process has four steps: a consultation, a simple integration with your online store or cashier system, the upfront funding, and then selling as usual. The FLIK team will give you a timeline during the consultation.
- **Text:** Can't find what you need? Talk to the FLIK team
