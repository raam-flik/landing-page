## Example stories (one per merchant, Raam, 12 Nov 2026)

Each brand has its own example story, written for its type of business. They are still examples: no quotes, names or numbers, and every page is labelled "Example story" until the brand approves its own. The text is in `STORIES` in both customers page scripts.

- **sigit-store** — The SIGIT sells its merch straight to fans with FLIK Checkout.
  - **Intro:** Sigit Store is the official store of The SIGIT, the rock band from Bandung. Fans buy shirts, records and tour merchandise on the band's own website.
  - **Challenge:** When new merch or a record drops, fans arrive all at once, mostly on their phones. A slow checkout or a long form at that moment means carts left behind just as stock runs out.
  - **How FLIK helps (checkout):** Sigit Store moved its checkout to FLIK. Fans log in with a WhatsApp code, their address fills in from their FLIK account, and they pay with QRIS, an e-wallet, a virtual account, a card or COD, all on one page.
  - **Result:** Drops sell through a checkout built for phones, and fans who bought once check out again in a few taps.
- **artch** — Artch gives its shoppers a checkout that keeps up with them.
  - **Intro:** Artch sells on its own website and wanted the last step of every order to feel as considered as the products.
  - **Challenge:** Shoppers who reach checkout have already made up their mind. Long forms, a redirect to another site or a missing payment method can still lose them at the final step.
  - **How FLIK helps (checkout):** With FLIK Checkout, Artch shoppers pay on one page. Returning shoppers are recognised from their FLIK account, so their details are already there, and they choose from QRIS, e-wallets, virtual accounts, cards and COD.
  - **Result:** The path from cart to paid is shorter, and Artch keeps the whole experience on its own website.
- **paper** — Paper keeps its checkout as clean as its brand.
  - **Intro:** Paper is a brand that lives on simple, bold design, and it wanted its website checkout to match.
  - **Challenge:** A cluttered checkout with too many fields works against a brand built on simplicity, and every extra screen gives shoppers a reason to stop.
  - **How FLIK helps (checkout):** Paper uses FLIK Checkout: one page, a WhatsApp code instead of a password, saved details for returning shoppers, and every popular payment method from QRIS to cards.
  - **Result:** Checkout feels as simple as the brand, and shoppers pay the way they prefer.
- **wakaigo** — WakaiGo turns more visits into orders with FLIK Checkout.
  - **Intro:** WakaiGo sells to a young, mobile-first audience on its own website.
  - **Challenge:** Its shoppers browse on their phones and expect to pay with the apps they already use. Typing an address and card number on a small screen is where many of them give up.
  - **How FLIK helps (checkout):** With FLIK Checkout, WakaiGo shoppers log in with a WhatsApp code, their details fill in, and they pay with QRIS or their e-wallet in a few taps. Cards, virtual accounts and COD are there too.
  - **Result:** Paying on a phone takes a few taps, and shoppers can use the payment app they already have.
- **osim** — OSIM makes buying wellness online feel effortless with FLIK Checkout.
  - **Intro:** OSIM brings wellness products, from massage chairs to personal care, to shoppers who buy online.
  - **Challenge:** A wellness product is a considered purchase. Shoppers want to pay with the method they trust, from a card to a bank virtual account, and expect the last step to feel as premium as the product.
  - **How FLIK helps (checkout):** OSIM uses FLIK Checkout for a one-page checkout with cards, virtual accounts, QRIS, e-wallets and COD. Returning shoppers are recognised, so their details are already filled in.
  - **Result:** Shoppers pay with the method they trust on a checkout that matches the brand, and returning customers buy again faster.
