The opportunity

Selling online takes more than a storefront. Every extra step between the cart and the customer's door costs you orders.

Long checkout forms, missing payment options and slow or expensive delivery turn interested shoppers into abandoned carts. And when a brand is ready to grow, cash tied up in inventory slows it down.

FLIK brings checkout, shipping and funding together on one platform. Shoppers pay in seconds, every order ships with the right courier, and your sales fund the next batch of stock, so you can focus on building your brand.
