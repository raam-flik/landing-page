## 1. Checkout

### How it works
- **Setup:** A brand owner sells on their own website (Shopify or WooCommerce) and wants a different checkout system. They use FLIK as their checkout.
- **Shopper flow:** The shopper adds items to the cart and clicks checkout, then gets redirected to the FLIK checkout page.
  - **FLIK session exists** (already registered): they go straight to the checkout page.
  - **New shopper:** they choose between **checkout as a guest** or **checkout as a FLIK user** (registering a FLIK account).
- **Why checkout as a FLIK user:** all shopper information is saved. Next time, on any website that uses FLIK checkout, they check out without hassle.
- **What the shopper fills in:**
  - full name
  - WhatsApp number
  - email
  - delivery address
  - courier (auto-selected)
  - payment method (auto-selected)
- **Login:** an OTP sent to WhatsApp, so there's no password.
- **Shipment origin:** the merchant's warehouse. Merchants manage it in **Settings → Warehouse**.
- **Money:** once the shopper has paid and the package has arrived, the money goes to the merchant's **balance (saldo)**.
- **Notifications and tracking for the shopper:**
  - an email at every status change
  - the **Shopper App**, which uses the same FLIK account, for notifications and order tracking

## 2. Shipping (FLIK Shipping)

### How it works
- **What it is:** FLIK is a **shipping aggregator**. Merchants send, track and manage shipments from the FLIK dashboard.
- **Shipment types:**
  - **Single:** 1 warehouse → 1 or many destinations.
  - **Bulk:** multiple warehouses → many destinations.
- **Payment:** the merchant pays in the dashboard before the shipment is created. If the balance (saldo) is too low, they top it up first.
- **Coverage:**
  - COD and non-COD.
  - Many courier services (layanan kurir): regular, same day, next day, instant, cargo.
  - Coverage: **across Indonesia**, nationwide (as in the FAQ).
- **Shipment creation success rate:** 99%.
- **Integrations:** sales websites built with Shopify and WooCommerce connect directly to the FLIK Shipping dashboard.

### Marketing copy
**Unbeatable Low Prices for Shipping!**
Ship for less up to 40% off! All couriers, real-time tracking, near-zero returns!

- **Big Discount & Cashback:** Ship your packages nationwide with crazy savings up to 40% off!

**Connected with website builders:** Your sales websites built with Shopify and WooCommerce will be directly connected to the FLIK Shipping dashboard so the delivery process becomes faster and easier.

### FAQ
- **What is FLIK Shipping?**
  A logistics service provider that helps business owners or individuals ship more affordably through one dashboard, with access to multiple couriers across Indonesia.
- **How does FLIK Shipping work?**
  You enter delivery addresses, in bulk or one by one, and FLIK Shipping shows all available shipping rates. You then choose the service (regular, same day, next day, instant or cargo) and a pickup schedule.
- **How much does it cost?**
  It's free. There's no registration fee.
- **Do I have to pay the COD shipping fee upfront?**
  Yes. Merchants pay the shipping and COD fees in advance. The COD amount is credited after successful delivery.
- **How do I receive COD funds?**
  They're credited automatically once the transaction is completed.
- **Can I cancel a shipment?**
  Not once the courier has picked up the item.
- **What happens if the delivery fails?**
  - The courier tries again the next day, up to two attempts.
  - If delivery still fails, the package is returned.
  - Courier-direct services have no redelivery attempt: undelivered packages are returned immediately.
- **How long does pickup take?**
  Pickup happens after the AWB is generated:
  - regular: 3–5 hours
  - same day: 1–4 hours
  - instant: immediate pickup once the AWB is ready
- **How do I contact FLIK customer service?**
  Monday to Friday, 09:00–18:00 WIB:
  - Email: support@flik.co.id
  - WhatsApp: +62 812-9201-2668

## How the 3 products connect
- **Checkout → Shipping:**
  - Orders from FLIK Checkout ship through FLIK Shipping.
  - Merchants set couriers in Pengaturan Pengiriman.
  - Shipments start from the merchant's warehouse.
- **Checkout and Shipping → Balance (saldo):**
  - Checkout payments and COD funds land in the merchant's balance after delivery.
  - The same balance pays for shipments.
- **Merchant Cash Advance ← sales:** repayment is deducted automatically from product sales.
- **Shoppers:** one FLIK account works across every merchant that uses FLIK Checkout and in the Shopper App.

**Technical**
1. **Languages:** `hreflang` for `en` (at `/`), `id` (at `/id/`) and `x-default` (→ `/`). Every page has a self-referencing canonical URL.
2. **One H1 per page.** The current homepage has none.
3. **Structured data:**
   - Organization and WebSite on the homepage
   - Service on each product page
   - FAQPage on Shipping and Merchant Cash Advance
   - Article on blog posts
4. **Crawling:** `sitemap.xml`, with both languages, listed in `robots.txt`, plus an HTML `/sitemap` page.
5. **Linking:**
   - every page is at most 2 clicks from home
   - the 3 products link to each other
   - use case and industry pages link to the products they use
   - blog posts link to their product page
6. **Thin pages:** OTP Login, Warehouses and Auto Settlement can start as sections on their parent product page. The menu link jumps to that section; they become full pages once there's enough content.
7. **Cek resi results:** `noindex` the result pages. Only `/shipping/track` itself is indexed.
8. **Existing URLs:** the current site serves English under `/en/...`. Add 301 redirects from `/en/*` to `/*`.
9. **Speed:** WebP images with explicit sizes, and alt text in both languages.
