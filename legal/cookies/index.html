1.About this policy

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies include browser storage (such as localStorage) and requests to other companies' servers, which share your IP address with them.

This policy covers flik.co.id. The merchant dashboard has its own cookies: [[W-10: dashboard analytics and cookies]]. How we handle personal data in general is in our Privacy Policy. This policy is from PT Flik Teknologi Indonesia (“FLIK”, “we”).

Language. This page is in English for now. A Bahasa Indonesia version will follow. If the two versions differ, the Bahasa Indonesia version prevails.

2.What the website uses today

As of [[W-1: date]], the website:

  • sets no cookies of its own;
  • stores your cookie choice in browser storage (localStorage, under the key flik_consent). It holds only your Analytics and Marketing choices, a version number and the time you chose. It isn't a cookie, and it isn't sent to us or anyone else;
  • serves its fonts from flik.co.id itself. The fonts (Inter and JetBrains Mono, under the SIL Open Font License) are self-hosted, so no font request goes to Google or any other third party;
  • loads one script from a third party. The In-Store Payment page loads three.js, a 3D graphics library, from cdnjs.cloudflare.com (Cloudflare's public network). Your browser requests it directly, so Cloudflare receives your IP address and browser details. The request sets no cookies. [[W-50: self-host three.js, or keep this row]];
  • loads nothing from Calendly until you click. Contact sales opens our call-booking page on calendly.com in a new tab. There, Calendly's own cookies and privacy policy apply;
  • keeps a dataLayer list in the open page. It records only your cookie choice. It holds no personal data, is cleared when you leave, and isn't sent anywhere. It's there for a future Google Analytics (GA4) tag, which isn't added yet and will load only after you turn on Analytics.

Our website host also receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page, in its server logs. [[W-8b: website host and log location]]

5.Your choices

  • On your first visit, a banner asks for your choice. Analytics and Marketing stay off until you turn them on.
  • Change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings button in the website footer. It reopens the banner, where you can turn Analytics and Marketing on or off. Strictly necessary items stay on.
  • Withdrawing consent stops new analytics or marketing data from being collected. It doesn't undo what was collected before.
  • Your browser can also block or delete cookies and browser storage. If you clear or block browser storage, we can't remember your choice, and the banner asks again.

6.Transfers outside Indonesia

Google (Analytics, once added and only if you consent), Cloudflare (the script in section 2), Calendly (if you open it) and other providers may process data outside Indonesia. We handle these transfers as described in our Privacy Policy, section 7.
