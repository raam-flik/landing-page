Built for brands that sell together. When a sale belongs to more than one business, everyone gets their share as it happens.

Set it once, split every payment. A Split Rule decides who gets what. FLIK applies it to every completed payment.

01Link walletsLink the wallets of your partners. Each is a verified FLIK merchant, and FLIK approves every link.
02Create a Split RuleGive each recipient a fixed amount in Rp or a percentage.
03FLIK splits itWhen a payment completes, the net amount is split. Your partners are paid first; the rest stays with you.
04Everyone withdrawsEach wallet has its own balance and statement, and withdraws to its own bank account.
