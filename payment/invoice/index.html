Create and send

From draft to sent in four steps. Everything happens in the FLIK dashboard. No code needed.

01Pick a customerChoose a saved customer, or add a name, WhatsApp number and email.
02Add productsFrom your product catalog. Prices fill in.
03Set the termsDue in 7, 14, 30 or 60 days, and the payment methods you accept.
04Review and sendSend it now, or schedule a send date.
  • Check before you sendThe amount, due date and email, all on one screen.
  • The methods you chooseQRIS, e-wallets, bank virtual accounts and cards, per invoice.
  • Copy their finance teamAdd CC recipients to the invoice email.
  • Send it now, or laterSchedule a send date, or keep it as a draft until you're ready.
  • No matching by handEach invoice is paid on its own page, so FLIK marks it Paid for you.
